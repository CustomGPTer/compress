The first release of Compress, a free video compressor for Android.

- Makes videos smaller without a difference you can see, at the full picture size unless you choose a smaller one
- Keeps going with the screen off or the app closed
- Quality levels, or a size to fit for WhatsApp, Discord, Gmail or a size of your own
- HEVC, H.264 and, where the phone supports it, AV1
- Trim, crop, rotate, speed, join, GIF and audio only
- No adverts, no accounts, nothing uploaded

**Download `Compress.apk` below.** It needs Android 10 or later on a 64-bit phone. Chrome may say the file might be harmful; it shows this for app files that do not come from Google Play. Choose Download anyway.

The full list of what is in this version, and how to install it, is in the [release notes](https://customgpter.github.io/compress/releases.html).
