Compress 1.1.1 joins TorusApps.com.

- The in-app More apps list is gone: More apps opens torusapps.com.
- The update check reads the signed TorusApps catalogue; Update available opens Compress's page and nothing downloads inside the app.
- Website and privacy links go to torusapps.com.
- A new opening screen, and About shows the developer and hello@torusapps.com.

**Download `Compress.apk` below.** It needs Android 10 or later on a 64-bit phone and installs over 1.1, keeping everything.

`compress-encoder-sources.zip` is the source code of the FFmpeg, x264 and x265 encoder that is built into the app, provided under the GNU General Public License. You do not need it to use the app.
