Release notes

What changed in each version of Compress. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.1 16 November 2026Latest

  • Four quality levels, each showing the size to expect before you choose: Visually identical, High, Balanced and Small.
  • Size targets for WhatsApp (16 MB), Discord (10 MB) and Gmail (25 MB), or a size of your own, with a second attempt if the first lands over.
  • Original resolution kept by default, with 2160p down to 240p or a custom figure, and a lower frame rate if you want one.
  • HEVC, H.264 and, where the phone's video chip can write it, AV1.
  • Two engines: Fast, on the phone's video chip, and Max quality, using the x264 and x265 encoders.
  • Compressing with the screen off or the app closed, with a progress notification and a finished notification that offers Share and Compare.
  • Up to three videos per go, one after another, with the size and time predicted before you start.
  • Videos that are already small, or that would come out no smaller, are skipped.
  • Trim, crop, rotate, mirror, speed, and volume from silent to three times louder.
  • Join up to three clips, make a GIF, or save the sound on its own.
  • Date and details kept on the copy, a switch to remove the location, and HDR kept or converted.
  • A compare screen with a divider to drag and pinch to zoom.
  • A home screen with all videos and albums, filters, sorting, and a total of the space that could be freed.
  • Copies saved to Movies/Compress by default, with other folders in Settings.
  • Replace originals, off by default, which uses Android's 30-day bin after each copy has been checked.
  • Overnight mode and automatic mode, both off by default.
  • Pausing when the phone is hot, and waiting for the charger under 15% battery.
  • An optional space-saved tally, off by default, with a Clear button.
  • Picking up the queue again after a restart.
  • A notice when a newer version is out.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.1 is called Compress.apk. It is 15.2 MB (15,199,962 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

952ee0f6aa535e5743486784ca80a610f0aa8fac41bc8bb3e241a2f4d0ae4425

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Compress.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
