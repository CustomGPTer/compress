Free in Compress, with adverts or a price elsewhere

Compress has no paid version, no subscription and no adverts. Other well-known video compressors pay their way with adverts, and some keep part of what they do for a paid version.

Download for Android

Free. Android 10 or later, 64-bit. What it does

Which videos it works on

Compress opens the kinds of video a phone can play, including MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM and 3GP, and always saves an MP4. Proton's listing names MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WebM, TS, 3GP, M4V, WMV and MPEG. Panda's listing says it converts between MP4, AVI, MKV, FLV, RMVB, 3GP, MPEG, WebM, WMV and MOV.

Where the work is done

Compress reads and writes your videos on your phone, with or without an internet connection, and no video is ever sent anywhere. Both other apps' listings also say the work is done on the phone. If it matters to you, check each app's privacy policy to see what else it collects before you give it a video.

What Compress does not do

  • It takes three videos per go and does them one after another. There is no unlimited batch.
  • It always saves an MP4. It is not a converter to MKV, WebM, AVI or other kinds of file.
  • It does not show a short sample before it starts. You compare afterwards, with the original still in place.
  • Compressing always discards something. The top two settings are chosen so that you should not see it, but no setting gives back exactly what went in.
  • Max quality is slow and warms the phone, and it does not keep HDR: an HDR video is compressed with the Fast engine instead.
  • AV1 is offered only on phones whose video chip can write it.
  • It needs a 64-bit phone or tablet with Android 10 or later. There is no version for iPhone or for computers.
